# Privacy Policy

Claude RTL Fix does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The application operates entirely locally on your machine. It reads and modifies files within the Claude desktop application directory and writes a small activity log to your local disk (`C:\ProgramData\ClaudeRTLFix\log.txt` on Windows, `~/.local/state/claude-rtl-fix/log.txt` on macOS). No information is sent to any server. No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting of any kind is included.
